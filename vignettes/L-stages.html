Staged L

A Staged Development Model for Immature Mosquitoes

The stages model of aquatic mosquito ecology tracks development through six compartments: eggs (\(E\)), four larval instars (\(L_1\)–\(L_4\)), and pupae (\(P\)). Each larval instar has its own maturation rate, density-dependent maturation delay, density-independent mortality, and density-dependent mortality. All density-dependent effects act through a weighted mean crowding index.

Differential Equations

Let \(\eta\) denote the egg laying rate from the adult mosquito component and let

\[L = w_1 L_1 + w_2 L_2 + w_3 L_3 + L_4\]

be the weighted mean crowding index. The fourth instar serves as the reference (\(w_4 = 1\)); setting all \(w_i = 1\) recovers unweighted total larval density.

The dynamical system is:

\[ \begin{array}{rl} dE/dt & = \eta - (\psi_E + \phi_E)\, E \\[4pt] dL_1/dt & = \psi_E E \;-\; \psi_1 L_1\, e^{-\xi_1 L} \;-\; (\phi_1 + \theta_1 L)\, L_1 \\[4pt] dL_2/dt & = \psi_1 L_1\, e^{-\xi_1 L} \;-\; \psi_2 L_2\, e^{-\xi_2 L} \;-\; (\phi_2 + \theta_2 L)\, L_2 \\[4pt] dL_3/dt & = \psi_2 L_2\, e^{-\xi_2 L} \;-\; \psi_3 L_3\, e^{-\xi_3 L} \;-\; (\phi_3 + \theta_3 L)\, L_3 \\[4pt] dL_4/dt & = \psi_3 L_3\, e^{-\xi_3 L} \;-\; \psi_4 L_4\, e^{-\xi_4 L} \;-\; (\phi_4 + \theta_4 L)\, L_4 \\[4pt] dP/dt & = \psi_4 L_4\, e^{-\xi_4 L} \;-\; (\phi_P + \psi_P)\, P \end{array} \]

The maturation flow leaving instar \(i\) is \(\psi_i L_i e^{-\xi_i L}\); it feeds directly into the next compartment. Density-dependent delays reduce maturation through the exponential term; density-dependent mortality increases linearly with \(L\).

The emergence rate of adult female mosquitoes is:

\[\alpha = \psi_P P\]

Equilibrium Solutions

Equilibrium values can be computed numerically given an egg deposition rate \(\eta\) using steady_state_L.stages. With \(\xi_i = 0\) for all instars, the system is quasi-linear and the solver converges reliably from the starting guess \(\eta \mathbf{1}_6\).

In the common case where all four instars share the same parameters (\(\psi_i = \psi\), \(\phi_i = \phi\), \(\theta_i = \theta\), \(w_i = 1\)), the instar densities are equal at equilibrium (\(L_1^* = L_2^* = L_3^* = L_4^*\)) and the flow through the system is set by the egg hatching rate and the target emergence rate. The density-dependent mortality slope \(\theta\) can then be treated as the parameter that calibrates the steady-state emergence rate to a desired value.
